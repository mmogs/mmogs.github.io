BMI Calculator

This is a website that calculates your Body Mass Index, which uses a persons height and weight to estimate their total body fat and shows if you are at a healthy weight. Since this formula just compares height to weight, and not other factors like gender, muscle mass, or fat distribution, it can be innacurate and you could still be healthy even if it calls you overweight. All information on this website is just a rough estimate, and you should ask your doctor to confirm this information.
